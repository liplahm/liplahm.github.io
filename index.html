I am a research intern at the Centre for Applied Research in Electronics (CARE), IIT Delhi, working on quantum communication in a programme funded by CDOT, IIT Madras. My research sits between quantum information theory and machine learning, with a focus on deep unfolding and graph-based algorithms, for example in information reconciliation for continuous-variable quantum key distribution.

I completed my M.Sc. in Physics at IIT Delhi, with a thesis on machine-learning-based post-processing of CV-QKD advised by Prof. Neel Kanth Kundu and Prof. Bhaskar Kanseri, and a B.Tech. in Engineering Physics at IIT Bombay (2024). I am currently applying for PhD positions in machine learning, algorithms and quantum information.

Research interests

  • QKD post-processing: information reconciliation, syndrome-based LDPC decoding, composable finite-size security for GG02.
  • Learned iterative algorithms: deep unfolding of belief propagation and min-sum decoders.
  • Quantum networks and fault tolerance: one-way quantum repeaters, CSS codes, magic-state supply.
  • Radar signal processing: off-grid joint range, velocity and angle estimation with FMCW arrays.
